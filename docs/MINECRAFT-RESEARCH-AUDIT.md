## Evidence basis and review method

I compared the original subsystem inventory in `docs/MINECRAFT-1.12.2-BASELINE.md` with each linked dossier, its reported artifact identities and class/resource evidence, reproduction steps, confidence labels, runtime claims, and stated unknowns. I also checked issue #9's seven handoff comments and current #2–#8/#18/#32/#33 states. Issues #2–#8, #32, and #33 are closed; #18 is open; #9 remains open. The six coverage assignments in #2 align to the client and integrated-server research surface. Excluded MVP features remain within research scope in the terrain, combat, items, and lifecycle reports.

The official client and server artifacts are identified in `MINECRAFT-1.12.2-BASELINE.md` by Mojang object SHA-1 and local SHA-256. Each dossier binds selected original class bytes to hashes; mapped MCP/Forge names and CFR output are used as navigation and reconstruction, not as recovered Mojang source. Forge `.2859` evidence is called out separately from vanilla. Reproducible registries and hashed resources in #32 provide independent checks for item coverage. Runtime evidence is limited to the exact vanilla client/server paths described below.

## Material gap and bounded follow-up

The static dossiers describe Forge hooks, capabilities, and lifecycle boundaries separately from vanilla, but the pinned Forge 14.23.5.2859 client repeatedly fails before title at `NetworkRegistry.newChannel`. A debugger also observed runtime `Side.class` as `BUKKIT` while mapped source presents `CLIENT`/`SERVER`; cause and relationship are unproven. This prevents evidence-backed claims about the actual mod-development runtime and affects future integration choices across several subsystems. I created [#34, Resolve the Forge 1.12.2 development-client startup blocker](https://github.com/BrantTanner/MarioCraft/issues/34), with exact reproduction/provenance criteria; #34 blocks #9 and #18. No behavior is inferred from the vanilla #33 run, and no implementation or Mario research was started.

## Evidence review result

This audit finds a published dossier for each subsystem assigned in #2, with source/artifact provenance, behavior or system maps, confidence limits, and reproduction/evidence references. This is a navigable coverage synopsis, not proof that every behavior is universal or exhaustively exercised. The reports identify non-exercised pathways and retain the archive-count discrepancy. #32 closes the finite registry/resource/mutation catalog gap, and #33 closes bounded connected-vanilla-client evidence, while explicitly leaving their documented unknowns. The release-wide material blocker is the Forge runtime provenance/startup issue tracked by #34.

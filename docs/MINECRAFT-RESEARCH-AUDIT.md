## Resolved follow-up and remaining boundary

Issue [#34](https://github.com/BrantTanner/MarioCraft/issues/34) is complete. Its evidence traces the three-value Side enum to Mergetool 0.2.3.2 annotation injection into MCP merge output; ForgeGradle transforms that class into the generated mapped runtime JAR, where NetworkRegistry.newChannel iterates CLIENT, SERVER, and BUKKIT although only the first two maps are initialized. Clean .2859 and .2864 MDKs reproduce the NPE. A hash-guarded script replaced only Side.class in the generated .2864 mapped JAR with the exact class from the SHA-1-verified official Forge universal JAR; payload comparison found 4,935 entries before and after and only that class differed. The uninstrumented repaired client reached title, created a disposable world, and saved/quit. See the [diagnosis report](https://github.com/BrantTanner/MarioCraft/blob/main/docs/issue34/MINECRAFT-FORGE-STARTUP-ISSUE34.md) and [repair script](https://github.com/BrantTanner/MarioCraft/blob/main/docs/issue34/repair-generated-forge-side.ps1).

This proves the diagnosed generated-artifact repair and the exercised .2864 title/world-save path. It does not prove untouched ForgeGradle launch success, end-to-end selection of the later MCP --inject false configuration, MarioCraft mod hooks, or third-party mod compatibility. Those lifecycle/build integration checks are assigned to #18 before any prototype; the remaining bounded vanilla/runtime paths in #3–#8 stay explicitly recorded in their dossiers. The current evidence shows no missing Minecraft subsystem ownership requiring another research follow-up.

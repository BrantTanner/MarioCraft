# Mario 64 collision, surfaces, health, and hazards: aggregate audit (#12)

**Disposition:** the research acceptance criteria for #12 are supported by the bounded investigations #42–#46 and the pre-existing #41 wall-kick work. This dossier reconciles those results for crossover implementation. It does not claim exhaustive runtime coverage, universal per-level surface reachability, or a completed playable integration. The parent issue remains responsible for implementation and its Minecraft gameplay seam.

## Target and evidence basis

All Mario evidence targets Super Mario 64 USA rev 0, 8 MiB ROM SHA-256 `17CE077343C6133F8C9F2D6D6D9A4AB62C8CD2AA57C40AEA1F490B4C8BB21D91`, observed in Project64 `3.0.1.5664-2df3434` (executable SHA-256 `C257C662D4E0B2CE1732B7F4A7D5DD4682F604CB082BBDBFCE5181470D6DBFB8`). Static claims are tied to exact target bytes and their published ranges/hashes; source-navigation labels are aids, not proof of shipped behavior. Runtime evidence comes from read-only Project64 sampling and bounded input scenarios. Counter-bracket equality is a consistency check, not an atomic snapshot or a physics-frame count. Raw ROM, RAM captures, saves, and local analysis artifacts are not published.

## Crossover-facing rules

- Keep Mario world coordinates in Mario units through collision and movement; apply the chosen world scale at the Minecraft boundary. Do not equate the 37-unit object hitbox radius with the 24/50-unit wall-query radii or the 30/60/150-unit probe offsets. Use the target floor/wall/ceiling query and quarter-step outcomes to disable Minecraft auto-step and resolve block contact. Discrete step and ledge behavior still needs implementation-level observation.
- Treat surface ID, floor class, geometry, object/platform motion and sound classification as separate inputs. For example, decimal 41 is hexadecimal `0x29`, a terrain-sound selector rather than a physics class. Slippery classes and water/lava branches have distinct target consumers. Moving-platform transforms belong to object behavior (later issue #14), not surface-force classification. Do not assign a force or effect to all 147 candidates based on labels; preserve unknown/unresolved rows.
- Represent health as the target integer value: cap `0x880`, death sentinel `0xFF`, queued damage/healing in `0x40` steps, with the HUD showing `(health >> 8)` up to eight segments. Preserve action, floor type, velocity, height/depth, cap flags and timer gates around damage. Schedule these updates on the Mario simulation cadence; do not assume one Minecraft tick equals one physics update. The observed water loss threshold is 140 units below water level; non-snow resurfacing healing is `0x1A` per updater iteration. Fall branches use peak-height delta 1150/3000 units plus velocity/action/floor/helper gates; the shipped 600-unit comparison is unreachable due to the zero-extended action width and must not be treated as a live threshold.
- Water entry evidence uses a strict 100-unit submergence condition, separate from the health system's 140-unit depth threshold. The `D2` stroke action's exit predicate and the observed water-minus-80 idle height lack a proven static clamp explanation. Preserve those as open behavior details. Lava surface type `0x01` and burning-action handlers have distinct damage paths; do not collapse every fire/contact effect to the lava-floor queue.
- HUD rendering is issue #13 territory. Lives, death/respawn and persistence follow issues #28 and #16. Keep health, life count, world preservation and save restoration as separate state. Coins/hearts/caps and course-specific content may be excluded from the MVP while their documented health effects remain available to research and later design.

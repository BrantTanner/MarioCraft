**Audit basis:** original open issue #13 and all six issue-thread comments; the five child dossiers and manifests/scripts; and plan [MARIO64-ISSUE13-PRESENTATION-PLAN.md](MARIO64-ISSUE13-PRESENTATION-PLAN.md). The child findings were audited against the original acceptance wording, not inferred from their closed status.

**Judgment:** root independently reviewed and passed this audit; all five original research criteria are supported at the bounded, representative scope explicitly established by the parent and child execution contract. The documented residuals are material coverage limits, but none prevents an original criterion from being met: the criteria ask for system tracing/inventory, representative runtime scenarios, a reproducible local workflow, and explicit accounting of remaining coverage. They do not require exhaustive runtime trials for every mode/resource or audible waveform capture. This is an evidence judgment, not a claim of exhaustive game coverage. The report and plan are published; parent issue #13 remains open with its original acceptance text/check boxes untouched for the root's final update.

Across these studies, main-image address arithmetic `PC = 0x80245000 + ROM offset` is used only for validated ROM offsets `0x1000..0xE855F`. Outside it, evidence relies on exact unique whole-block matches or independently verified live-memory identity. Function envelopes may include internal Ghidra body gaps and are identified as envelopes; body byte counts are kept distinct. No global mapping is inferred from a local match. Hashes establish byte identity for the named ranges/outputs; they do not by themselves prove behavior. Runtime field reads are sampled and bracketed, not atomic. The target ROM, decoded assets, raw memory/traces, emulator state files, screenshots and private fixtures remain local.

For the publication-boundary check, root inspected the complete GitHub repository tree at commit `fa2ca4f54912a4434cc4960d611fbb7d882d3036`: 114 entries, `truncated=false`. No `tools/` paths or files with ROM/game-asset extensions such as PNG, WAV, OBJ, EEP, PJ, or ZIP were present in that snapshot. This is a snapshot of that commit, not a claim about every historical tree or about future changes.

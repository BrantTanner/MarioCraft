**Status: OPEN.** The [original issue](https://github.com/BrantTanner/MarioCraft/issues/15) retains all five unchecked acceptance criteria. [#10](https://github.com/BrantTanner/MarioCraft/issues/10) is closed; [#57](https://github.com/BrantTanner/MarioCraft/issues/57) and [#58](https://github.com/BrantTanner/MarioCraft/issues/58) are closed; [#59](https://github.com/BrantTanner/MarioCraft/issues/59) remains open. This is an accounting of current evidence and remaining work, not closure. The [plan](MARIO64-ISSUE15-COURSE-SCRIPTS-PLAN.md) records dependencies and scope.

## System map and evidence boundaries

The pinned [USA source](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/tree/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af) maps course/level definitions to dispatcher entries and level scripts; those declare shared and level-specific loads, areas, geometry, terrain, object placement and warp nodes. Source interactions and game state then select objectives, caps, cannon and transition paths. The source declares intent; source-encoded cartridge correspondence supports bounded data interpretations; native code windows support bounded semantics; a passive snapshot supports only the state actually sampled. None alone proves a transition or every route. A segmented pointer is not a general cartridge offset.

[#57 report](MARIO64-ISSUE57-COURSE-LEVELS.md) and [manifest](MARIO64-ISSUE57-COURSE-LEVEL-MANIFEST.json) inventory 26 course rows (15 main, sentinel, 10 bonus), 38 level rows (31 definitions, seven stubs), 33 script files, 143 level-script arrays, eight entry/dispatcher arrays, 18 shared families and 52 areas. All 52 geometry and 47 terrain references resolve; five cinematic areas lack terrain declarations. The 42 macro tables have 1,528 placements and 42 separate terminators; unresolved table/local-script targets are zero in scope. BOB has a unique bounded command/bank correspondence with placement, warp and spawn operands, not observed VM execution.

[#58 report](MARIO64-ISSUE58-COURSE-ASSET-LOADING.md) and [manifest](MARIO64-ISSUE58-ASSET-LOAD-MANIFEST.json) cover 79 valid MIO0 streams, 290 active source load/execute uses, 31 source-encoded course banks and 235 encoded entry-load commands. MIO0 linker-order pairings are candidates, not independent rebuilt-source byte equality; some shared/raw payload identities remain unresolved. A passive BOB sample matched 14 of 15 full blocks; one segment-7 byte differed and was located at a macro-object parameter position; its cause was not established. A later controlled pause-exit observed BOB-to-castle level change and selected segment replacements; exact allocation/free timing and continuous lifetimes remain unobserved.

[#59 objective report](MARIO64-ISSUE59-OBJECTIVES-CAPS.md) and [objective inventory](MARIO64-ISSUE59-OBJECTIVE-INVENTORY.json) contain 105 rows: 90 named objectives across 15 courses plus 15 separate 100-coin stars. Red-coin objectives are among the six named acts, not extra stars. Star ordinal 1–7, native index 0–6 and act selection 1–6 or null are distinct. Every named row has a USA-active same-index placement candidate and behavior annotation; this is not universal route, prerequisite, ordering or reachability proof. The [cannon inventory](MARIO64-ISSUE59-CANNON-INVENTORY.json) records 31 source declarations. Secret/castle stages, switches, cap loss/recovery, keys, ending and related source paths are covered with stated limits in the report.

The [warp report](MARIO64-ISSUE59-WARPS-AND-STATE.md) and [inventory](MARIO64-ISSUE59-WARP-INVENTORY.json) account for 311 USA declarations: 258 normal, 44 painting, nine instant. All have bounded encoded cartridge matches: 187 at source-corresponding entry positions and 124 uniquely matched local commands. Destination declarations do not prove spawn availability or reachability. The [native manifest](MARIO64-ISSUE59-WARP-NATIVE-MANIFEST.json) covers 35 inspected warp function windows; cap and star-collection native cross-checks are separately documented in the objective report. These establish static roles, not execution.

## Original acceptance criteria and disposition

- [ ] **Inventory courses/areas, level/geometry/object scripts, segmented addresses, asset containers/compression and loading transitions.** #57 provides the finite source census; #58 provides load categories, candidate containers, encoded bounds and a passive snapshot. Shared payload identity and exact lifetime timing retain the limits documented in closed #58.
- [ ] **Trace stars, objectives, caps/cap loss, cannons, warps, castle/course transitions and progression state even though these are MVP exclusions.** #59 provides finite source/encoded inventories and bounded native paths. Route prerequisites, ambiguous associations, reachable execution and representative transition families remain open in #59.
- [ ] **Verify representative script/data interpretations against Project64 observations and preserve source-bound asset inventories.** #57's bounded BOB cartridge check and #58's passive BOB loaded snapshot support representative interpretations, with the single-byte distinction above; source-bound inventories are linked here. #58 subsequently recorded a controlled BOB-to-castle transition and selected segment comparisons; #59 retains its distinct progression-family runtime requirements.
- [ ] **Document what is loaded dynamically and how its runtime state relates to cartridge offsets.** #58 identifies source declarations, encoded bounds, loader semantics and one passive state. The selected #58 transition provides bounded before/after correspondence; broader lifetime timing remains unknown; do not extend #10's initial linear mapping to arbitrary segments.
- [ ] **Produce full coverage accounting and bounded follow-up issues for unexamined content families.** The finite inventories and limits above account for their declared scopes. #59 owns the remaining bounded progression gaps; full runtime coverage is not established. No duplicate follow-up is needed.

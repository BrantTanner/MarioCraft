# MarioCraft issue 46: water and lava runtime evidence

This report combines a reproducible water-entry route recipe with one observed entry and swimming trial, plus a directly sampled Lethal Lava Land floor-contact transition, for the exact Super Mario 64 USA rev0 image. The sanitized machine-readable records are [water evidence](mario64-issue46-water-runtime-evidence.json), [lava evidence](mario64-issue46-lava-runtime-evidence.json), and the [artifact manifest](mario64-issue46-evidence-manifest.json). Both runtime records identify ROM SHA-256 `17CE077343C6133F8C9F2D6D6D9A4AB62C8CD2AA57C40AEA1F490B4C8BB21D91` and the isolated Project64 executable SHA-256 `C257C662D4E0B2CE1732B7F4A7D5DD4682F604CB082BBDBFCE5181470D6DBFB8`.

## Water

The single captured water-entry trial starts from the verified issue35 courtyard save, with Mario at `(-1328,260,4354)`, and follows the bounded route recorded in the JSON. At sequence 406 the sampler reads `Y=-185.50542`, `waterLevelS16=-81`, and floor type 21; the next sample has action `0x300022C2` with previous action `0x300022E2`. A separate 240-row settling capture reaches `Y=-161`, 80 units above the sampled water level, in action `0x380022C0`, while floor type remains 21. This demonstrates that the water-level scalar and floor-type scalar are distinct observations. The `-80` vertical offset is directly observed; this work did not establish the exact-USA-ROM code path that clamps Mario to it.

The single entry sample is below `waterLevel-100` (`-181`); the exact-USA-ROM entry branch at PC `0x80269830` (ROM offset `0x24830`) uses a strict less-than comparison. The separate derived-input branch at PC `0x80253A60` uses input bit `0x0200` and `Y < waterLevel-10`; it is not the entry threshold. Do not generalize either one-sample observation into a broad threshold sweep.

From the observed water-idle state, one 300 ms requested A press (actual key edges are in the JSON) enters numeric action `0x300024D0`; measured forward speed rises from `0.5` to `10.0` with horizontal velocity. The samples then show `0x300024D1`, with `previousAction=0x300024D2` on sequence 40, then `0x300022C2` and settled `0x380022C0`. Thus the trace brackets a brief `0x300024D2` state but does not contain a sampled row with that current action. Exact-ROM code at PC `0x80271AA0` (ROM `[0x2CAA0,0x2CD04)`, SHA-256 `737CAAC9C4C17ADC9015FD40DE54D112F85656B0637A5A94D631EF877312FBC2`) increments the action timer and selects `0x300024D2` at timer 14 when Mario flag bit `0x4` and input bit `0x2000` are clear. It also resets the timer to 1 when the A-latched state is set at timer 9. The sampled sequence is consistent with this timer handler, but this work did not prove the subsequent `0x300024D2` exit predicate or a direct `0xD2`-to-`0xD1` transition. Numeric IDs are retained rather than assigning source-only action names.

## Lava contact

The lava trial begins from the issue35 baseline, enters the basement by normal movement, and reaches the LLL painting. Several temporary isolated save-flag fixture attempts during courtyard and basement preparation were reverted by Project64. The final successful basement-door crossing used only the reviewed one-byte write of `0x40` to `0x8020770B`, after the observed flags word was zero. Project64 restored that byte when cheats were disabled. Cheats were confirmed off before the course capture; this preparation was not natural progression, and no cheat was active during the measurements.

The course-entry sample is in area 1 with water-level sentinel `-11000`. At sequence 3784, Mario is at `Y=10`, floor height `0`, and floor type `1`; at sequence 3785, he reaches the floor and action changes to `0x04000471`. The next sample (sequence 3786) has floor type 1, `Y=84`, vertical velocity `80.8`, previous action `0x04000471`, and numeric action `0x010208B7`. That value appears in 274 sampled observations from sequence 3786 through 4059, spanning 5.932 seconds of host time. Loop-counter pairs span 112725–112891 and repeat; the final 20 observations share counter 112891. Therefore, 274 samples do not mean 274 simulation frames, and the duration or number of burn updates is not established. `floorPointer` stayed `0x801A2420` throughout this interval; later candidate surface-type reads at that same pointer differ, for an unknown reason, and are excluded from the contact claim. `areaPointer` was `0x8033B90C` with area-index byte 1 during contact; at sequence 4060 it changed to `0x8033B984`, area-index byte 3, at the castle return position. The HUD showed 4 lives at course entry and 3 after respawn. No health field was measured, so the life change is not reported as a health decrement.

Exact USA rev0 code independently corroborates the numeric transition. Floor-type-1 dispatcher PC `[0x802507FC,0x8025093C)` (ROM `[0xB7FC,0xB93C)`, SHA-256 `FADDFD232507344B53E1F18B414C0C14F4D77DF89AAC71407A28E1F587058795`) calls helper PC `[0x8025065C,0x80250724)` (ROM `[0xB65C,0xB724)`, SHA-256 `0994CF3AF657AE36A7FE11D917A877B2006211C9A8D02075A2A9B4A6DB1F0226`) when its action-flag gates are clear. The helper requires `Y < floorY+10`, then calls the action setter with numeric action `0x010208B7` under its own flag gates. The observed floor type, Y, and following numeric action match this consumer. Its `MarioState+0xB2` byte update is not established as health and is not assigned that meaning here.

## Reproduction, provenance, and limits

The water procedure and all sampled rows are in the water JSON. Its entry trace SHA-256 is `6029EEF1BEDF4811253E4B5ACC03AE7909E9E8C79EA22F3D2C00D925E0D44D6A`; settling trace `18A137C5C9E30FF62D72A957352511DC88B092C32643D9663D458D94A60065A1`; stroke trace `4B737CC0A2926798A5356CEC32189D5D40EAF99FA6939FFB34221172CF111454`. The lava record gives the exact six completed helper input events, including the sign-dismissal and final movement pulse; its local full trace SHA-256 is `5C9A9181995C0D0BF8B7EA886411A8461A524718C9C504FE91B32F9D3533721A` (4,671 rows). The helper event log and raw traces remain local.

The lava capture was executed by `tools/issue11-local/pj64-portable/issue46-basement-door-entry-capture.ps1` (SHA-256 `34D93E10D372E1F65B68BD148122A27259D6F5093EF73D7EDAB89E027A4834C8`). An exact-byte copy for reproducibility is included as [mario64-issue46-basement-door-entry-capture.ps1](mario64-issue46-basement-door-entry-capture.ps1) with the same hash. The similarly named `docs/research/mario64-issue46-external-capture.ps1` (SHA-256 `89C992D17E6740D8B407EAA11F885BEDE85A48809D5252390B92B8E6C58BC645`) is a related template and was not executed. The area-index byte is read from the current area pointer at guest offset `+0`; pinned `struct Area` navigation identifies the signed index at that offset, used here only to distinguish course area 1 from castle area 3.

These are bounded trials, not repeated water-entry runs or a sweep of water levels, burn timing, damage, or contact angles. The `waterLevel-80` surface offset is runtime evidence only; an exact ROM clamp mechanism was not proved. The `0x80271AA0` timer conditions are exact-ROM evidence, but the exit predicate from `0x300024D2` to `0x300024D1` was not established. The loop counter brackets external reads but does not make them atomic. Late candidate floor-type reads are inconsistent despite the same pointer, so only contact-time type-1 samples are used for the lava claim. No ROM, source game assets, original saves, or raw trace data are included in the sanitized records.

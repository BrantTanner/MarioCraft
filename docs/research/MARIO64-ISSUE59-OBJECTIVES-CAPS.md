Touching a cannon base while not already in the cannon selects `ACT_IN_CANNON`, stores the object, and stops holding/riding. The action waits for the cannon object, then applies stick input to pitch and yaw (clamped to `0..0x38E3` and `-0x4000..0x4000` respectively). A press computes horizontal/vertical launch speed from 100 and the aim, moves Mario 120 units along aim, sets `ACT_SHOT_FROM_CANNON`, and advances the cannon object action. These are source semantics, not observed inputs or trajectory. See [interaction.c](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/blob/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af/src/game/interaction.c#L1066-L1075) and [mario_actions_automatic.c](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/blob/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af/src/game/mario_actions_automatic.c#L674-L765).

## Bowser and ending

The [Bowser death state](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/blob/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af/src/game/behaviors/bowser.inc.c#L1360-L1393) reaches different ending routines for the first two arenas and the final arena. After the first two defeat dialogs and twirl, it spawns a key; after the final defeat dialog, it spawns a grand star ([collectable selection](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/blob/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af/src/game/behaviors/bowser.inc.c#L1161-L1172), [routines](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/blob/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af/src/game/behaviors/bowser.inc.c#L1275-L1349)). Key collection follows the level-specific flag rule above. A grand-star subtype selects the jumbo-star action ([interaction](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/blob/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af/src/game/interaction.c#L826-L829)); its flying sequence reaches `WARP_OP_CREDITS_START` at action timer 500 ([cutscene](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/blob/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af/src/game/mario_actions_cutscene.c#L1933-L1955)). Delayed credits start/next walk the source credits sequence; the final waving action issues credits-end ([credits](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/blob/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af/src/game/mario_actions_cutscene.c#L2652-L2673), [warp dispatch](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/blob/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af/src/game/level_update.c#L841-L868)). Credits-end requests special result `-1`; the global dispatcher selects the ending script, which loads area 1, fades in, calls the end-screen sound and loops ([dispatcher](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/blob/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af/levels/scripts.c#L120-L135), [ending](https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64/blob/9921382a68bb0c865e5e45eb594d9c64db59b1af/levels/ending/script.c#L18-L36)). The source script does not establish an observed cake display or player execution; both remain unobserved.

The collection handler's save helper `0x80279C44` is a unique aligned match at ROM `0x34C44`, 512 bytes, SHA-256 `0E8F1E0AA6E5E3238126533DDECB0347770F7F3EC0C2F688A5FFD87BFF9DE509`. This supports the bounded source mapping, not a claim that EEPROM was written or a save was exercised. See #16 for persistence.
